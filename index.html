One row per owned ticker. The quality score now uses the Institutional Quant Lens as a first-class input, then layers thesis support, source freshness, coverage, and risk penalties. Review priority is shown separately so urgency does not masquerade as quality.

WhatEvidence-ranked holdingsOwned tickers are scored for review quality, not trade execution.
WhySeparate durable quality from urgencyA loud signal is not the same thing as a disciplined long-term holding.
NextInspect the gapsOpen Factors & rank to inspect support, missing data, factor checks, and risk penalties.

Evidence Quality Ranking Decision support

Quality score = Institutional Quant Lens + academic factor discipline + thesis + source quality, with penalties for weak data and concentration risk. Review priority is a separate urgency read; neither score predicts returns or places trades.
